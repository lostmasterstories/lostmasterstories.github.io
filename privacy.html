Privacy policy

This policy covers "Lost Master Stories" (the "App"), an internal tool used only by the owner of the Lost Master Stories YouTube channel to manage that channel. The App is not offered to the public.

What the App does

The App uses the YouTube API Services to upload videos, thumbnails, titles, descriptions and comments to the owner's own YouTube channel, and to update that channel's description, keywords and banner.

Data the App accesses

With the channel owner's authorization (Google OAuth), the App accesses only what it needs to manage that channel: the channel's ID and branding settings, and the videos it uploads. It does not access, collect or store data about any other YouTube user, viewer or commenter.

How data is stored

The OAuth access token is stored only on the owner's computer and is never shared with third parties. The App does not sell, rent or transfer any data. No analytics, advertising or tracking tools are used by the App or this website.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. When access is revoked, the stored token stops working and is deleted.

YouTube and Google

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
